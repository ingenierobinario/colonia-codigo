**The problem:** you've built a project that works (agents with their roles, skills, routines, Flows) and a partner,
client or colleague wants the same. Explaining it takes hours, and sending your files drags your keys along.

**With Colonia:** you export the project to a file, copy it as text or publish a link. The other person sees what it
brings before importing anything. Your keys, chats, memory and tasks never leave your computer.

## 1 · Share it

<Steps>

1. In the project's menu, choose **Share project**.
2. Check what is shared (instructions, agents, skills, connections without their keys, team structure, Flows) and what
   never leaves your computer (keys and passwords, chats, memory, tasks, channels and their sessions).
3. If needed, click **Choose project files** and tick specific documents.
4. Choose how:
   - **Export › Save to a file**, or **Copy as text** to paste it in a message.
   - **Publish › Create temporary link**: Colonia opens a free link, no account or install needed. It works while your
     Colonia is on and changes when you restart it. **Copy link** and send it.

</Steps>

If the text had something that looks like a key, Colonia removes it from the shared copy and tells you how many it
removed and where they were. Your project stays as it was.

## 2 · The other person imports it

<Steps>

1. When adding a project, under "Did someone send you a project?", click **Import project**. Pick the file or paste the link or text.
2. **See what it brings**, then **Import**.
3. Whatever needs a connection shows under **Needs connecting**: they add their own keys with **Connect** and it works.

</Steps>

Flows and routines arrive **switched off**: nothing runs until the other person reviews and turns them on. If the
project consulted other projects they don't have, Colonia lets them link those to their own right there.

<Aside type="tip">
Agents and skills from Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Gemini in the project folder show up in it and travel when you
share it too.
</Aside>

## Just the steps: a recipe

Don't want to hand over the whole project, just something your agent already did well (say, signing up a client on a
website)? In the chat's menu, **Export as a recipe**: it saves a `.md` with the steps. The other person imports it with
**+ Skill › Recipe**, chooses whether their agent does it live (they see the pointer move) or in the background, and
clicks **Import recipe**. Importing only reads it: nothing runs until they ask their agent to.
