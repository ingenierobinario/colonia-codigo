**The problem:** you've built your team on your home computer (projects, agents, routines, channels) and now you want
it on your laptop too. Or you're changing computers and don't want to start from scratch.

**With Colonia:** two ways. **Sync computers** keeps two computers the same, live. **Backup & transfer** makes an
encrypted backup that you restore on another computer. Both are in **My computers** and in **Settings**.

## Way A · Two computers that look the same

Projects, agents, Flows, AIs, channels, apps and browsers. What you change on one shows up on the other.

<Steps>

1. On the computer that's already set up, open **Settings › Sync computers**, choose **Show a code here** and click
   **Create code**. You get an 8-character code (like `ABCD-2345`) that works once.
2. On the other computer, with Colonia open, go to the same place, choose **I have a code**, type it and click
   **Link**.
3. The two find each other on your network and talk directly, encrypted. The state turns to up to date.

</Steps>

- WhatsApp and the other single-session channels stay on the first computer; the other one sees their conversations.
- If one computer is off, the other waits and catches up on its own when it's back. If both change the same thing,
  nothing is lost: it's kept in the saved versions.
- The computer that creates the code keeps running your channels and routines, so nothing runs twice.

## Way B · Move it to a new computer

<Steps>

1. On your usual computer, open **Backup & transfer** and choose a password (at least 8 characters; without it the
   backup can't be opened and there's no way to recover it). Click **Make backup**.
2. If you have projects whose folder lives outside Colonia, tick **Include documents and code from my external
   projects**.
3. Take the `.colonia` file to the new computer (USB stick or your cloud). If you save it in a OneDrive, iCloud Drive
   or Dropbox folder, it will already be there.
4. On the new computer, open **Backup & transfer**, pick the backup, type its password and click **See what it
   brings**. Before anything changes you see how many projects, chats, agents, routines and channels it brings.
5. Choose **Replace** or **Merge** (what's missing is added and, where they match, the new computer's version stays)
   and click **Restore**.

</Steps>

Projects, chats, Flows, tasks, routines, knowledge, AIs with their keys, agents, channels and your settings travel.
The WhatsApp, iMessage and Signal sessions and the logins in browsers and apps stay on the source computer: when it
finishes, Colonia tells you what to link again and takes you to **Channels**.

<Aside type="tip">
First time opening Colonia on the new computer? The welcome assistant asks whether you already use it somewhere else
and lets you link the two or recover a backup from the start.
</Aside>
