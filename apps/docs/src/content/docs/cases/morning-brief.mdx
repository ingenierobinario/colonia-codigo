**The problem:** you start the day with dozens of unread emails and chats. Three of them matter (a client who says
yes, another asking for an invoice, one who says no) and you have to read everything to find them. And sometimes your
follow-up list says one thing and your sent mail says another.

**With Colonia:** at the time you choose, an agent reads what came in since the last check, compares it with your
tasks and leaves you a short brief: what changed, what is pending and what needs your decision. It sends nothing on
its own.

## 1 · Connect what it should read

- **Email**: Gmail in **Resources › Connections**, or another provider in **Settings › Channels › Email**. Step by
  step in [Email inbox](/cases/email-inbox/).
- **WhatsApp** or another messaging app, if your clients write to you there: [Work from your
  phone](/cases/mobile/).

## 2 · Give an agent the job

<Steps>

1. Create a project for this work (for example, **Clients**) or use one you already have.
2. In **Agents**, add one with a clear role: "Client manager. You read client email and chats, keep each client's
   tasks up to date and tell me what needs my decision".
3. In **Tasks**, keep one task per client or deal, with its state. That is the list the agent checks what it reads
   against.

</Steps>

## 3 · Schedule the brief

<Steps>

1. In the project, open **Routines** and click **New routine**.
2. **Agent**: the manager you just created. **When**: **On a schedule**, Monday to Friday at **09:00**.
3. Write the brief:

   > Read client email and chats since the last check. Compare them with the tasks: tell me if a task says "pending"
   > and the email shows it is done, or the other way round, or if an amount changed. Give me the brief in three
   > blocks: changed, pending, needs my decision. Send nothing. If a source fails, say so.

4. **Save**, then click the run button to try it now.

</Steps>

## 4 · Decide in minutes

The brief reaches you in the app and, if you pick it in **Settings › Interruptions › Chat app for notices**, on your
phone. Reply right there: "Draft the follow-up to the hosting brand with the new price". The agent leaves it as a
**draft**; you review it and send it.

<Aside type="tip">
What the agent learns about each client stays in the project's memory, so tomorrow you don't explain anything again.
If one day it can't read a source, the brief says so instead of filling the gap.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution">
For the routine to run on time, the computer must be on with Colonia open (or running in the background).
</Aside>
