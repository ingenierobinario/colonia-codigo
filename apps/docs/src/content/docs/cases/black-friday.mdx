The example office is **Brezo Ceramics**, a small shop with four agents: Lucía (manager), Tomás (web), Inés
(marketing) and Pablo (customer service). The campaign: Black Friday inside the Christmas launch.

## 1 · Ask for the plan

In the chat with Home, say what you want in one sentence:

> Plan the Christmas launch, with the Black Friday campaign in week 3.

<Shot name="case-launch" alt="The chat with the secretary: the request for the launch plan and the answer with a table by week and person" caption="The plan, by week and by person" />

The secretary answers with a plan by weeks, creates the tasks and assigns each one to the right agent. If something
doesn't fit, say it: "Move the landing page to week 1".

## 2 · Check the team

<Steps>

1. Open the project (**Brezo Ceramics** in the side bar).
2. In **Agents**, check who is in and what each one does. **Areas and roles** shows who reports to whom.
3. If you are missing someone (a designer, for example), add them from there.

</Steps>

<Shot name="team" alt="The project's Agents tab and then Areas and roles" caption="Agents and Areas and roles" />

## 3 · Follow the work

<Shot name="project" alt="The project: overview, chats and tasks" caption="Overview, Chats and Tasks" />

- **Overview**: how the project is doing at a glance.
- **Chats**: each agent's conversation; open one to see what they are doing or to ask them something.
- **Tasks**: the tasks of the plan, with who has each one and its state. **New task** adds one by hand.

When an agent needs your OK (publishing the landing page, sending the newsletter), it arrives in **For you**. Nothing
goes out without you if you set it up that way.

<Aside type="tip">
For the day itself, ask Inés for a routine: "Every day of the campaign at 19:00, tell me sales and the best-selling
product". How to make it: [Weekly report](/cases/weekly-report/).
</Aside>
