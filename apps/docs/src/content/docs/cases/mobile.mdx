**The problem:** to know what's going on you have to be at the computer. If you're recording, travelling or with a
client, either you drop everything or the rest waits. And when a scary notice arrives ("an unknown user logged into
your account"), you lose the morning working out whether it's serious.

**With Colonia:** you write to your team on WhatsApp (or Telegram, Slack, Discord, iMessage, email…) and it answers
there. Its notices reach you, you ask it for things and you forward what you don't understand so it checks for you.

## 1 · Give it a WhatsApp number

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings › Channels**, use **Add channel** and pick **WhatsApp**.
2. Use a second number only your agent uses and leave **This line is my own phone** off: your own chats stay
   yours.
3. Press **Link** and, on that number's phone, **WhatsApp › Settings › Linked devices › Link a device**. Point it at
   the QR code.

</Steps>

You can link more than one number: the **+** menu in Channels always offers every channel, including a second account
of the same one. If the computer sleeps, WhatsApp reconnects by itself when it wakes. Details in the [WhatsApp
channel](/channels/whatsapp/).

<Aside type="tip">
Prefer Telegram, Slack or Discord? Connecting them is a guide with one step per screen: each step opens the right page,
copies what you need to paste, and at the end Colonia checks the connection and tells you what to fix.
</Aside>

## 2 · Get notices on your phone

In **Settings › Interruptions › Chat app for notices**, pick WhatsApp. From then on the morning brief, pending
approvals and anything urgent reach your phone, within your quiet hours and your interruption limit.

## 3 · Use it like a person

Some requests that work well from the phone:

> What do I have today and what is waiting for my reply?

> Draft the reply to yesterday's client: we confirm Thursday.

> I got this from my social network, have I been hacked? *(and paste or forward the notice)*

The secretary knows your projects and uses your connections to check before answering. Faced with a notice like that,
it looks at your accounts and contacts and tells you plainly what it is, whether it affects you and what to do.

<Aside type="caution">
Never send it passwords or codes by chat. Accounts are connected in Settings, not by message.
</Aside>
