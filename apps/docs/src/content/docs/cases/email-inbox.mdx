The goal: you stop opening your email to triage it. Colonia reads the new emails, tells you only the important ones
and an agent prepares the reply as a **draft** for you to check and send.

## 1 · Connect the mailbox

Two ways, pick one:

| You use | Connect it in | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Gmail** | **Resources › Integrations › Gmail** | Read, search and drafts in your own Gmail. |
| Any other email | **Settings › Channels › Email** | Sending by SMTP; add the channel's IMAP to receive. |

<Shot name="resources-integrations" alt="Resources, Integrations: the list of services you can connect, Gmail among them" caption="Resources › Integrations" />

<Steps>

1. Open **Resources** in the side bar and go to **Integrations**.
2. Open **Gmail** and follow the dialog. If your company asks for its own app, choose **My own OAuth client**: the
   dialog walks you through it.
3. In **Configure integration**, turn on the review of new emails and choose how often and whether only the important
   ones.

</Steps>

## 2 · Let the secretary tell you

With the [Secretary profile](/cases/secretary-first-day/) on, new emails show up in your day: in the morning brief
and, if something can't wait, as a notice that goes through your interruption budget. In **Settings › Super-agent ›
Secretary capabilities**, the line about new emails has a shortcut, **Open integrations**, that takes you to the same place.

## 3 · Replies as drafts

Ask in the chat, to Home or to any agent:

> Prepare a short reply to the supplier's email: we confirm the order for Thursday.

The agent writes a **draft**; it doesn't send it. You open it in your email, change what you want and send it. If you
use a signature per sender, it is added on its own ([signatures](/office/email/)).

<Shot name="inbox" alt="The Inbox: each agent's chats with their last message, one after another" caption="Inbox: what each agent did" />

Everything your agents did is in **Inbox**, one chat after another, so you can see who prepared which reply.

<Aside type="caution">
Don't paste passwords or keys in the chat. Email is connected in Resources or in Settings, never by message.
</Aside>
