**The problem:** a campaign has ten pieces (landing page, emails, posts, customer service) and you are the one who
remembers who has each. **With Colonia:** the secretary turns one sentence into a plan with tasks and owners, each
agent does its part and you only approve what goes out.

The example office is **Brezo Ceramics**, a small shop with four agents: Lucía (manager), Tomás (web), Inés
(marketing) and Pablo (customer service). The campaign: launching the winter collection.

## 1 · Ask for the plan

In the chat with Home, say what you want in one sentence:

> Plan the winter collection launch over four weeks, with a sale week in week 3.

The secretary answers with a week-by-week plan, creates the tasks and assigns each one to the right agent. If
something doesn't fit, say so: "Move the campaign page to week 1".

## 2 · Check the team

<Steps>

1. Open the project (**Brezo Ceramics** in the sidebar).
2. In **Agents**, see who is there and what each one does.
3. In **Areas and roles**, put each agent in an area (Marketing, Web, Support) and give it a role. The org chart shows
   who reports to whom and what each one is responsible for.
4. If someone is missing (a designer, say), add them from there.

</Steps>

## 3 · Follow the work

<Shot name="project" alt="The project: overview, chats and tasks" caption="Overview, Chats and Tasks" />

- **Overview**: how the project is doing at a glance.
- **Chats**: each agent's conversation; open one to see what they are doing or to ask them something. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>
  + click opens it in its own tab, and you switch between agents at the top, like in an editor.
- **Tasks**: the tasks of the plan, with who has each one and its state. **New task** adds one by hand.

When an agent needs your OK (publishing the page, sending the newsletter), it arrives in **For you**. If it asks for
several approvals in a row while you are away, it doesn't sit waiting: all the cards wait for you and you approve
them when you're back.

<Aside type="tip">
For the sale week, ask Inés for a routine: "Every day of the sale at 19:00, tell me sales and the best-selling
product". How to make it: [Weekly report](/cases/weekly-report/).
</Aside>
