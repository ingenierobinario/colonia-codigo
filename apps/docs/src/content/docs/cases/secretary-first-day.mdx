By the end of this guide your super-agent opens and closes your working day, watches your work every few hours and
only writes to you when something is worth it. You need Colonia installed and an AI connected
([First steps](/start/first-steps/)).

## 1 · Give it the Secretary profile

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>,</kbd>, or your name at the bottom left) and go to **Super-agent**.
2. Under **Profile**, pick **Secretary**. **None** turns the profile routines off; **Focus coach** is the other
   profile that comes with Colonia.
3. Check the times it brings: it opens the day at 08:30 and closes it at 18:30 on weekdays, and a look at
   your work every 2 hours. Change any of them right there.
4. Go to **Interruptions** in the same window.

</Steps>

<Shot name="case-secretary" alt="Settings: the Super-agent section with the Secretary profile, then Interruptions" caption="Settings › Super-agent, then Interruptions" />

## 2 · Decide how often it may interrupt you

Everything Colonia says without being asked goes through **Interruptions**. For a first week these values work well:

| Setting | Start with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Unasked messages per day** | 3 | Enough for what matters; the rest waits in **For you**. |
| **Quiet hours** | On, 21:00 to 08:00 | What it would say at night arrives together in the morning. |
| **Gap per project (minutes)** | 180 min | Two notices about the same project don't arrive back to back. |

The open and the close of the day always arrive and never count towards the cap. The full list of settings is in
[Secretary](/office/secretary/#the-interruption-budget).

## 3 · Your first morning

Write to Home in the chat, from the app or from your chat app:

<Shot name="home-hero" alt="The chat with the secretary: a question about today and the answer with the day's priorities" caption="Home answers with your day" />

<Steps>

1. Ask **"What's on my plate today?"**. It answers with what is due, what is waiting for you and where to start.
2. Answer as you would to a person: **"Yes, tell Lucía I'll look at the plan after lunch"**. It passes the message on
   and leaves you a reminder.
3. Open **For you** in the top bar: it is the only place where everything that needs you lands (an OK, a task
   waiting for you, something due).

</Steps>

## 4 · Teach it what is worth it

Unasked messages arrive with no buttons. React to them: 👍 means useful, 👎 means not. Two 👎 on the same kind of
notice in 14 days and it stops sending it until a 👍 turns it back on. In **Interruptions › What it meant to send** you
can see every one and what happened to it.

<Aside type="tip">
You can also ask it in the chat: "Don't write to me after 20:00". It shows the change before and after and waits for
your OK in the app.
</Aside>
