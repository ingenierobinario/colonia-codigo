Colonia runs on your computer and keeps your chats, agents, files and keys there.

## What leaves your computer

- **To your AI provider**: what each request needs, such as your messages and the context or files the agent uses. What it
  does with them is governed by its terms: review them.
- **To the services you connect**: if you connect Telegram, WhatsApp or other channels, messages go through them. If an
  agent browses, the websites it visits receive those visits.
- **Update checks**: every few hours Colonia checks whether there is a new version. `APX_UPDATES=off` turns it off.

## Telemetry: off by default

Colonia has no telemetry destination configured, so it sends usage statistics to nobody. Even if the app shows a question
about usage statistics, without a destination nothing leaves your computer.

Data is only sent if someone sets the `APX_TELEMETRY` variable to a destination on the computer where Colonia runs.

## If a destination is set

The install steps are sent (installed, opened, setup finished, first use, updated), with a random ID, the version, the
operating system, the processor and the install type. If you also agree to share usage statistics, a daily report is added
with total tokens, turns, and how many projects, agents, channels and routines you have.

Your messages and what agents write are never sent; nor are names of people, agents, projects or files; folder paths; which
models or providers you use; or your keys.

## How to make sure it stays off

<Aside>
Any of these turns everything off, even with a destination: `APX_TELEMETRY=off` in the environment or in `core.env`,
`DO_NOT_TRACK=1` or a `CI` variable.
</Aside>
