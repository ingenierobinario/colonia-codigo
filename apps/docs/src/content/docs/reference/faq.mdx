## The basics

### What is Colonia?

A team of AI agents that works from your computer: a secretary, Home, and agents per project. More in the
[Introduction](/start/introduction/).

### Do I need to know how to code?

No. You use it from the app, talking to the agents. Terminal commands are optional.

### Do I have to install anything?

Yes, on your macOS, Windows or Linux computer. See
[Install](/start/install/).

### Does the computer have to be on?

Yes: agents and routines work while Colonia is running. If you want it always on, you can install it on
[your own server](/devices/server/).

## The AI and its costs

### Does Colonia include the AI?

No. You connect your subscription, your key or a model on your computer. See [Connect the AI](/start/connect-ai/).

### What costs are there on top?

Whatever your AI provider charges, which you pay directly. With a model on your own computer there is no provider, but you
need a powerful machine.

## Data and control

### Where is my data?

On your computer. The AI provider and services you connect receive what each request needs. More in
[Privacy](/reference/privacy/).

### Does Colonia send statistics?

No, unless someone sets a destination: telemetry comes switched off.

### Can they do things without me knowing?

You decide what they do directly and what needs your confirmation in [Autonomy](/concepts/autonomy/). Passwords and
payments in the browser come back to you. AI can be wrong: review what matters.

## If something breaks

### My system says the app is not safe

That is expected: the apps are not signed yet. [Install](/start/install/) explains how to go on.

### Something does not work

Start with [Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting/).
