Colonia does not include the artificial intelligence: without an AI connected, the agents cannot work. You connect yours
and pay your provider directly, not Colonia.

## What your options are

| Option | What it is | What you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ChatGPT plan | You use the subscription you already pay for | To sign in with your account |
| A provider's key | You pay for what you use | A key from Gemini, Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq, Cerebras, DeepSeek, Mistral or OpenCode Zen |
| Model on your computer | The AI runs on your machine, with Ollama or LM Studio | To install it; no key, and it needs a powerful computer |

## Which to choose

If you already pay for ChatGPT, start there: it is the simplest. If not, a provider's key lets you pay only for what you
spend. Models on your computer are for people who want nothing to leave their machine and have a powerful computer.

<Aside type="caution" title="If you use Claude">
The Claude subscription only works inside Claude Code. For Colonia's agents to use Claude directly you need an Anthropic
key, paid separately from the subscription.
</Aside>

## How to connect it

You do it in the first-run wizard, on the **Engines** step, or later in **Config › Engines and models**.

- **ChatGPT plan**: click sign in and follow the instructions. First turn on **Device code authorization for Codex** in
  ChatGPT, under Settings › Security.
- **A provider's key**: get it from your provider's website, click the button to add a key and paste it.
- **Model on your computer**: install Ollama or LM Studio; it appears as an engine without asking for a key.

## Which model is used

You can connect more than one and order them with the arrows in the **Model chain**: the first is the one used and, if it
fails, the next takes over. You can also change the model in a single chat. More in [Models](/concepts/models/).

## Watch your spending

With a key, each message costs according to what the model uses, and routines and agents also spend when they work alone.
If your provider allows it, set a spending limit there before giving the agents more autonomy.
