The first time you open Colonia, a short wizard sets up your team. It takes a few minutes, and everything can be changed
later in Config.

## The setup wizard

<Steps>

1. **Welcome.** Choose to start from scratch.

2. **Your secretary.** That is Home: it coordinates the rest and looks after your channels. Give it a name and pick its
   face.

3. **Engines.** Connect your AI. If you are unsure, first read [Connect the AI](/start/connect-ai/).

4. **Memory.** If in doubt, choose **Search by words**: it needs nothing else.

5. **Channels.** Optional, and you can leave it for later. Connect a Telegram bot or link WhatsApp with a QR code, just
   like WhatsApp Web.

6. **Projects.** Add a folder now or leave it for later.

7. **Done.** Open Colonia.

</Steps>

## Say hi to Home

Home's chat is in the inbox. Write as you would to a person and press Enter.

<Shot name="home-hero" alt="The inbox with Home's chat open and a short exchange with the secretary" />

Try something real:

- "What can you do for me?"
- "Remind me to call Northwind about the invoice on Friday at 10."
- "Open my shop's website and tell me what the home page says."

## Add a project

A project is a folder where your agents work: a shop's, a client's or your company's. In the sidebar, click **New
project** and choose the folder, or create it without one and Colonia keeps it in its own.

The project appears with its tabs: Summary, Chats, Agents, Tasks, Routines, Files, Memory and Resources. Under **Agents**
you can create one or bring one from a template. More in [Projects](/concepts/projects/).

## Decide how much they do alone

Before handing over important work, review [Autonomy](/concepts/autonomy/): there you decide which actions they take
directly and which need your confirmation. Start low and work up. AI can be wrong: review what matters.
