Colonia is a team of AI agents that works for you from your computer. You have a secretary that knows you and agents
that look after each project: you ask for things in your own words, as you would a colleague, and you decide what they
can do alone and what needs your approval.

<Shot name="home-hero" alt="The inbox with Home's chat open: a short conversation with the secretary" caption="Home, your secretary, in the desktop app" />

## What you need

- **A computer** with macOS, Windows or Linux. Colonia installs on it, and the agents work while it is running.
- **Your own AI.** Colonia does not include the artificial intelligence: you connect your subscription or key, and you pay
  your provider directly. See [Connect the AI](/start/connect-ai/).

You do not need to know how to code.

## Who is who

- **Home** is your secretary. It answers you in the app and on your channels, keeps your commitments in mind and hands
  work to the rest of the team. What you write on Telegram lands in the same conversation as what you write in the app.
- **Project agents** work in each project's folder, with a name, a role and instructions. Lucía can run a shop's launch
  while Tomás fixes the checkout and Vera drafts the newsletter.
- **The browser** comes inside the desktop app. Agents open tabs, read pages and fill in forms; you can take over at any
  time, and passwords, codes and cards stay with you.

## Where to talk to them

From the desktop app, your Android phone, and Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, Slack or iMessage. If you like the terminal,
also with the `apx` command. It is all the same colony, so nothing gets out of sync.
